# Privacy

Plenty Flow keeps everything you make on your device.

- **No account.** There is nothing to sign in to.
- **No server.** The app never contacts one. There is no analytics, no crash reporting, no ads.
- **Your flows, studios, movements and calendar** are stored in the app's own storage on the device,
  and in the backups it makes in the Files app (On My iPhone → Plenty Flow → Plenty Flow backups). If
  you have iCloud Backup turned on for the device, Apple backs these up as it does any app's data.
- **Hours and pay**, if you turn them on, stay on the device like everything else.
- **Sharing by QR code** sends one flow (or studio, or backup) straight from one device to the other
  over your own Wi-Fi. It is sealed with a key that exists only in the code on screen, works once,
  and stops after ten minutes. Nothing goes over the internet.
- **Syncing your own devices** (up to 6) also stays on your Wi-Fi. Pairing gives your devices a key
  that only they hold; everything they send each other is sealed with it. Backup files never
  contain that key. A device you stop syncing keeps what it has.
- **Sharing by file** hands a file to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes after that is up to you.

## What the app asks permission for

- **Camera**: only to scan a sharing or pairing code. Pictures are not kept.
- **Local network**: to share a flow by QR code, and to find and sync with your own devices.
- **Notifications**: only if you turn reminders on, to remind you before a shift and the night before
  a class with no flow.
- **Calendars**: only if you turn on "Add my shifts to Calendar". Plenty Flow makes a calendar of its
  own called Plenty Flow and fills it with your shifts. It doesn't read or change your other
  calendars. Turning it off removes that calendar.

## Removing everything

Settings → Erase everything, or delete the app. Backups you copied elsewhere are yours to delete.
